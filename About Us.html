About Us

Discover who we are, what we believe, and our desire to see lives restored through Jesus Christ.

EST. 2018

Welcome to Christ Life Restoration Ministries

Christ Life Restoration Ministries is a Christ-centered ministry established in 2018 with a desire to share the love of Jesus Christ and bring hope, healing, encouragement, and restoration to people's lives.

We believe that every person has value and purpose in God's plan. No matter what someone has experienced or what season of life they are currently facing, there is hope through Jesus Christ.

Our heart is to create a welcoming community where people can worship God, learn His Word, grow in faith, receive prayer, and serve others.

Our Mission

Our mission is to proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, teach the truth of God's Word, encourage people in their faith, and help individuals and families experience spiritual restoration.

We desire to reach people with the message of salvation, strengthen believers, support those going through difficult seasons, and encourage everyone to live a life that honors God.

Our Vision

Our vision is to see transformed lives, restored families, strong communities, and people who are passionate about following Jesus Christ.

We envision a growing church family where people discover their God-given purpose and become a positive influence in their homes, communities, and wherever God sends them.

What We Believe

  • We believe in one God, the Creator of heaven and earth.
  • We believe in Jesus Christ, the Son of God and Savior of the world.
  • We believe that salvation is found through faith in Jesus Christ.
  • We believe the Holy Spirit works in the lives of God's people.
  • We believe the Bible is God's Word and is foundational to Christian life.
  • We believe in the power of prayer, worship, fellowship, and service.
  • We believe God gives people hope, purpose, and the opportunity for restoration.
  • We believe Christians are called to love God and love others.
