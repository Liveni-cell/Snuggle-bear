Our Services

Come and worship with us, grow in God's Word, build meaningful relationships, and experience the love of Christ.

Worship, Grow & Serve

At Christ Life Restoration Ministries, we believe that church is more than a place to attend. It is a family where we worship together, learn God's Word, pray together, encourage one another, and serve our community.

Everyone is welcome. Whether you have been following Christ for many years or you are simply searching for answers and hope, there is a place for you here.

Our Ministries

🙏 Prayer Ministry

We believe in the power of prayer. Our prayer ministry provides an opportunity for people to come together, seek God's guidance, intercede for others, and bring their needs before the Lord.

🌟 Youth Ministry

Our youth ministry seeks to help young people develop a strong relationship with Jesus Christ, discover their purpose, build positive friendships, and grow in God's Word.

🌷 Women's Ministry

A place for women to encourage one another, grow spiritually, pray together, build friendships, and support one another through life's different seasons.

🛡️ Men's Ministry

Our men's ministry encourages men to grow in faith, become stronger spiritual leaders, support their families, and serve God and their communities.

❤️ Community Outreach

We seek to demonstrate the love of Christ beyond the church through acts of kindness, community support, evangelism, and service to those in need.

🕊️ Pastoral Care & Counseling

We provide spiritual encouragement, prayer, and pastoral support for people facing difficult seasons, family challenges, grief, and other life circumstances.

Come and Worship With Us

Come as you are. Come with your questions. Come with your burdens. Come with your hopes and dreams.
